## Diagnosis
1. **The signal has negative short-term direction.** On 503 train candidates the index moved *against* the trade over the
   next 30 minutes: mean -3.3 pts (-0.10 ATR), hit rate 45% vs 52% unconditional. The edge-triggered entry fires after
   a move has extended, and the move then partly reverts. This is the main loss driver; option decay and costs add to it.
2. **No put trades at all.** The monthly RSI was below 50 on only 57 of 1,225 days (range 43.5..83.2), so the
   monthly-RSI filter makes the strategy calls-only. The put side cannot be evaluated on this sample.
3. **Barriers do not fit the horizon.** 86-92% of trades exit at the 30-min time stop; a 30% premium stop and ~+65%
   target are rarely reached in 30 minutes.
4. Costs are about INR 110 per trade (charges still [VERIFY]); losses exist before costs.
